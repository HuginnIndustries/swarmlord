# Skill: Intake

Capture the raw idea, interpret the intent behind it, list assumptions, and surface open questions. Output goes into `spec/idea.md`.

## Steps

1. Restate the idea in the user's own framing.
2. Translate it into a one-paragraph "what this almost certainly means".
3. List assumptions you are making to fill gaps.
4. Flag every open question that would change the project's direction.
